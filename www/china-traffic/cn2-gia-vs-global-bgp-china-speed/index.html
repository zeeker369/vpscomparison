CN2 GIA vs Global BGP: Why Your China-Facing Site is Slow

If you are hosting a website for mainland China users, the difference between CN2 GIA and Global BGP is the difference between a functional site and a 30% bounce rate.

Intent score: 96

Start with the real decision

This comparison is not about raw bandwidth; it is about routing priority and reliability through the Great Firewall.

Bottom line:
Choose CN2 GIA (AS4809) if you want stable, low-latency access during China's "peak hours." Choose Global BGP only if your budget is extremely tight and you don't mind significant packet loss (up to 30%) for mainland Chinese users.

Quick decision

  • Best for: Businesses with 50%+ traffic from mainland China, High-stakes APIs and transaction-heavy sites, Teams tired of "peak hour" slowdowns (9 PM Beijing time)
  • Less ideal for: Global sites with negligible China traffic, Projects strictly optimizing for the lowest possible cost, Static blogs that don't mind 5-second load times
