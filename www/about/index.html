About this site and how we check prices

We compare VPS plans you can open today: Hostinger, Vultr, and DigitalOcean. Prices are read in the cart every month. Last checked 1 Aug 2026.

What this site is

An independent buying guide. The money path is three providers you can sign up for without a sales call: Hostinger KVM, Vultr High Performance Cloud Compute, and DigitalOcean Basic Droplets. Region is a filter after checkout. Singapore and Tokyo guides stay for that filter. They are not the first decision.

Some outbound buttons are affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you. There is no Hetzner cash affiliate on this site, so Hetzner is a Europe mention only, not a homepage button.

On the 1st of each month we open the Hostinger, Vultr, and DigitalOcean carts and compare the same RAM. The stamp on this page is always the 1st, not the day we happened to type. If the number did not change, we only move this date to the new month. If it did, we update the figure and the checked date on the money pages too. The cart always wins over a public marketing page.

  • Next check: 1 Sep 2026. A full pass still happens in January when years in titles need a look.
  • Hostinger KVM 1: 4 GB, about $5.19/month intro. Renewal about $11.99/month. The public page without a code may show about $6.49 intro.
  • Hostinger KVM 2 / 4: 8 GB about $8.99 intro; 16 GB about $17.99 intro. Renewal on those SKUs is in the cart.
  • Vultr High Performance and DigitalOcean Basic: 4 / 8 / 16 GB about $24 / $48 / $96 per month, billed hourly. Skip the $6 1 GB plan.

We do not invent USD prices. We do not treat 1 GB as a cheaper 4 GB. Live WordPress on this site starts at 4 GB.
