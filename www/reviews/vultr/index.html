What you actually buy

The homepage pick on this site is Vultr High Performance Cloud Compute at 4 GB, about $24/month. Disk on that line is 100 GB NVMe in the figures already published here. You pay for hours. Destroy the instance and billing stops.

Regular Cloud Compute 1 GB is about $5/month. High Performance 1 GB is $6. Those are smaller machines. Use them for a weekend SSH test, not a live WordPress site.

New-account credit (as published on this site: $300 in 30 days) cuts the first invoice. It does not change the catalog. Spin a 4 GB node, run the test, then decide against Hostinger’s prepaid cycle.

When Vultr is the right open

  • You might delete the server in weeks, so hourly beats a 12-month Hostinger lock.
  • You want a location Hostinger does not sell, and you will pick it after signup.
  • You are comfortable with a cloud console, not hPanel.
  • You already compared 4 GB to 4 GB, not $6 to $5.19.

When to open someone else

  • You want 4 GB and a panel at the cheap intro rate: Hostinger.
  • You want Droplets and managed databases later: DigitalOcean.
  • You want a live WordPress site with a GUI: start at Hostinger KVM 1, not Vultr 1 GB.
