What you actually buy

This site’s Vultr pick is High Performance Cloud Compute, not Regular Cloud Compute. The 4 GB HP line is about $24/month with 100 GB NVMe in the figures already published here. 8 GB HP is about $48/month. 16 GB HP is about $96/month. You pay for hours, capped at a month of hours. Destroy the instance and billing stops.

Regular Cloud Compute is cheaper on some RAM sizes and slower disks. Optimized / dedicated-CPU SKUs cost more than High Performance at the same RAM. If the cart shows a different family, you are not on the comparison in the table above.

New-account credit (as published on this site: $300 in 30 days) cuts the first invoice. It does not change the catalog. Spin 4 GB, resize to 8 GB for a WooCommerce test, then decide against Hostinger’s prepaid cycle.

When Vultr is the right open

  • You might delete or resize in weeks, so hourly beats a 12-month Hostinger lock.
  • You want a location Hostinger does not sell, and you need to confirm that location before checkout.
  • You are comfortable with a cloud console, not hPanel.
  • You already matched RAM to RAM, not $6 to $6.49, and not $48 to $8.99 without noticing billing.

When to open someone else

  • You want 4 GB or 8 GB and a panel at the cheap intro rate: Hostinger.
  • You want Droplets and managed databases later: DigitalOcean.
  • You want WordPress with a GUI: start at Hostinger KVM 1 (4 GB) or KVM 2 (8 GB), not Vultr 1 GB. Details on the WordPress VPS page.

Check region before checkout

Do not start with Singapore vs Tokyo. Open the account, then filter the node. Same rule as the homepage.

FAQs

How much is 4 GB, 8 GB, or 16 GB on Vultr?

High Performance list price is about $24, $48, and $96 per month, billed hourly. Check the cart.

Should I buy the $6 Vultr plan?

Not for a live site. That is 1 GB. Start at 4 GB.

When do I need 8 GB?

WooCommerce, heavy builders, or several sites. Hostinger KVM 2 is the cheap 8 GB intro if you will keep the box. Vultr 8 GB is about $48/month hourly.

Is Vultr cheaper than Hostinger?

If you might delete the box in weeks, yes. If it will run a year, Hostinger intro at the same RAM is cheaper even after you read renewal on KVM 1.

Does Vultr include a control panel?

A cloud console. You install the stack unless you add a marketplace app. Hostinger includes hPanel.

Should I check the region before checkout?

Yes, if latency or data location matters. Confirm the region before checkout.
