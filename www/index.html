VPS comparison FAQs

Short answers before you open an account.

Prices checked August 2026. Recheck in January 2027. Confirm the cart before you pay.

Which VPS is cheapest for 4 GB RAM?

Hostinger KVM 1. It is 4 GB RAM at about $4.99/month on a longer intro cycle. A 4 GB Vultr or DigitalOcean instance is about $24/month.

Is Hostinger $4.99/month the real price?

It is a real intro rate on a longer billing cycle. Renewal is higher. Check the renewal in the cart before you pay.

Why does a $6 VPS have less RAM than $4.99?

The cheap Vultr and DigitalOcean plans are usually 1 GB RAM. Hostinger KVM 1 at about $4.99 intro is 4 GB. Compare RAM, not the badge.

Is hourly billing cheaper than an annual VPS?

Hourly is cheaper if you might delete the server in weeks. Hostinger intro pricing is cheaper if the box will run a year and you can live with the renewal jump.

Should I choose Singapore or Tokyo before I pick a provider?

No. Open Hostinger, Vultr, or DigitalOcean first. Singapore, Tokyo, or US/EU is a location filter after the account exists.

Does Hostinger include a control panel?

Yes. Hostinger KVM plans include hPanel. Vultr and DigitalOcean give you a cloud console. You pick the image and install the stack yourself unless you add a marketplace app.

Is Hostinger or Vultr better for WordPress?

Hostinger if you want 4 GB RAM and a panel. Vultr if you want hourly billing and you will install WordPress yourself. Do not run production WordPress on 1 GB.

Do I get backups and enough bandwidth?

Entry plans are enough transfer for a small site. Backups and snapshots are often extra. Check the plan page in the cart before you pay.

Can I delete the VPS anytime?

On Vultr and DigitalOcean, destroy the instance and hourly billing stops. Hostinger intro pricing is a prepaid cycle, so you paid for that term up front. Read the refund rules in the cart.
