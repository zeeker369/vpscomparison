DigitalOcean Review 2026: Droplets vs Hostinger

Basic Droplets are about $24/month at 4 GB, $48 at 8 GB, $96 at 16 GB, billed hourly. Skip the $6 1 GB plan. If the box will stay up, Hostinger 4 GB is about $6.49/month intro and 8 GB KVM 2 is about $8.99/month intro.

Quick verdict

Open DigitalOcean when you want Droplets, a clear console, and hourly billing. Start at 4 GB for a live site. Move to 8 GB or 16 GB when RAM actually runs out. Do not treat the $6 sticker as a cheaper 4 GB plan.

Bottom line:
Compare the same RAM. Hostinger still wins on intro dollars per gigabyte. DigitalOcean wins if you might delete the Droplet in weeks, or you want managed databases later. What you actually buy

This site’s DigitalOcean pick is a Basic Droplet. 4 GB is about $24/month. 8 GB is about $48/month. 16 GB is about $96/month. Billing is hourly, per-second on Droplets, with a monthly cap. Destroy the Droplet and billing stops.

CPU-Optimized, General Purpose, Memory-Optimized, and Storage-Optimized Droplets start higher (CPU-Optimized 8 GB is $84/month on the public price list). If the cart shows Premium Intel/AMD or those families, you are not on the Basic ladder in the table. Use them when you already know you need dedicated CPU or extra RAM per vCPU, not as a “cheaper 8 GB.”

New-account credit (as published on this site: $200 in 60 days) cuts the first invoice. It does not change the catalog. Spin 4 GB, resize to 8 GB for a WooCommerce test, then decide against Hostinger’s prepaid cycle or Vultr hourly.

When DigitalOcean is the right open

  • You want Droplets and a console you already know.
  • You might add a managed database later, still on DigitalOcean.
  • You might delete or resize in weeks, so hourly beats a 12-month Hostinger lock.
  • You already matched RAM to RAM, not $6 to $6.49, and not $48 to $8.99 without noticing billing.

When to open someone else

  • You want 4 GB or 8 GB and a panel at the cheap intro rate: Hostinger.
  • You want Cloud Compute and a longer node list: Vultr.
  • You want WordPress with a GUI: start at Hostinger KVM 1 (4 GB) or KVM 2 (8 GB), not a 1 GB Droplet. Details on the WordPress VPS page.

Check region before checkout

Do not start with Singapore vs Tokyo. Open the account, then filter the Droplet region. Same rule as the homepage. A Singapore-specific note lives on the DigitalOcean Singapore page if you already have the account.

FAQs

How much is 4 GB, 8 GB, or 16 GB on DigitalOcean?

Basic list price is about $24, $48, and $96 per month, billed hourly or per-second. Check the cart.

Should I buy the $6 DigitalOcean plan?

Not for a live site. That is 1 GB. $4 is 512 MB. Start at 4 GB.

When do I need 8 GB?

WooCommerce, heavy builders, or several sites. Hostinger KVM 2 is the cheap 8 GB intro if you will keep the box. A Basic 8 GB Droplet is about $48/month hourly.

Is DigitalOcean cheaper than Hostinger?

If you might delete the box in weeks, yes. If it will run a year, Hostinger intro at the same RAM is cheaper even after you read renewal on KVM 1.

Is DigitalOcean or Vultr better?

Same 4 / 8 / 16 GB list prices here. DigitalOcean for Droplets and managed extras. Vultr for Cloud Compute and extra nodes. Compare the provider fit, then confirm the region before checkout.

Should I check the region before checkout?

Yes, if latency or data location matters. Confirm the region before checkout.
