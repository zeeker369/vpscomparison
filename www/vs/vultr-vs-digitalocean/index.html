Vultr vs DigitalOcean 2026: Hourly Billing, Nodes, and Which to Open

What you actually buy

This site’s Vultr pick is High Performance Cloud Compute, not Regular Cloud Compute. Regular is cheaper on some RAM sizes and slower disks. Optimized / dedicated-CPU SKUs cost more than High Performance at the same RAM.

This site’s DigitalOcean pick is a Basic Droplet. CPU-Optimized 8 GB is $84/month on the public price list, not $48. If the cart shows Premium Intel/AMD or a dedicated-CPU family, you are not on the table above.

Billing is hourly on Vultr, hourly / per-second on DigitalOcean Droplets, capped at a month of hours. Destroy the instance and billing stops. Hostinger is prepaid. New-account credits (as published here: Vultr $300 in 30 days, DigitalOcean $200 in 60 days) cut the first invoice. They do not change the catalog.

The real split is not price

Once you match RAM, the checkout question is locations versus platform extras.

  • Open Vultr when you want broader node coverage, High Performance compute, and you will install the stack yourself. Full size notes: Vultr review.
  • Open DigitalOcean when you want Droplets, a console you already know, or managed PostgreSQL / Redis / Kubernetes later. Full size notes: DigitalOcean review.
  • Open Hostinger when the box will stay up and you want hPanel. KVM 1 is 4 GB. KVM 2 is 8 GB. Do not treat $48 hourly as equal to the ~$8.99 intro.

Check region before checkout

Do not start with Singapore vs Tokyo. Open the account, then filter the node. Same rule as the homepage. Vultr usually has more cities to pick from. DigitalOcean covers the usual ones. Neither is a mainland-China default.

FAQs

Is Vultr cheaper than DigitalOcean?

Not on this site’s 4 / 8 / 16 GB pick. High Performance and Basic are about $24, $48, and $96. Confirm the cart. Regular Compute and CPU-Optimized are different SKUs.

Should I buy the $6 plan on either side?

Not for a live site. That is 1 GB. DigitalOcean $4 is 512 MB. Start at 4 GB.

When do I need 8 GB?

WooCommerce, heavy builders, or several sites. Hostinger KVM 2 is the cheap 8 GB intro if you will keep the box. Hourly 8 GB is about $48 on both Vultr and DigitalOcean.

When should I open Vultr?

Extra locations and Cloud Compute. Same RAM costs about the same as a Basic Droplet.

When should I open DigitalOcean?

Droplets, a known console, or managed databases later. Same 4 / 8 / 16 GB list as Vultr High Performance here.

When should I open Hostinger instead?

The box will stay up and you want a panel. 4 GB intro is about $6.49/month. 8 GB KVM 2 is about $8.99 intro.

Should I check the region before checkout?

Yes, if latency or data location matters. Confirm the region before checkout.
