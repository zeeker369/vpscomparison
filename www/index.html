Why does this comparison start at 4 GB RAM?

Hostinger KVM 1 is 4 GB at about $6.49/month intro. Vultr and DigitalOcean 4 GB plans are about $24/month. Skip their $6 1 GB plans. Linux, the panel, the database, and the site share RAM, so 1 GB is too small for a live WordPress site.

Which VPS is cheapest for 4 GB RAM?

Hostinger KVM 1. It is 4 GB RAM at about $6.49/month on a longer intro cycle. A 4 GB Vultr or DigitalOcean instance is about $24/month.

Is Hostinger $6.49/month the real price?

It is a real intro rate on a longer billing cycle. Renewal is about $11.99/month. Check the cart before you pay.

Should I compare the $6 Vultr or DigitalOcean plans?

No. Those are 1 GB. This page compares 4 GB to 4 GB. Hostinger KVM 1 is 4 GB. Vultr and DigitalOcean 4 GB plans are about $24/month.

When is 1 GB enough?

1 GB is enough for a short hourly test: learn SSH, run a tiny script, or try a node for a few days, then delete it. Use Vultr or DigitalOcean hourly for that. It is not enough for a live WordPress site or a server you keep online. This page still compares 4 GB to 4 GB.

Is hourly billing cheaper than an annual VPS?

Hourly is cheaper if you might delete the server in weeks. Hostinger intro pricing is cheaper if the box will run a year and you can live with the renewal jump.

Should I check Singapore or Tokyo before I buy?

Yes, if latency or data location matters. Use the Singapore or Tokyo guide before checkout, then confirm that your chosen provider offers the location you need.

Does Hostinger include a control panel?

Yes. Hostinger KVM plans include hPanel. Vultr and DigitalOcean give you a cloud console. You pick the image and install the stack yourself unless you add a marketplace app.

Is Hostinger or Vultr better for WordPress?

Hostinger if you want 4 GB RAM and a panel. Vultr if you want hourly billing and you will install WordPress yourself. Start at 4 GB for a live site.

Do I get backups and enough bandwidth?

Entry plans are enough transfer for a small site. Backups and snapshots are often extra. Check the plan page in the cart before you pay.

Can I delete the VPS anytime?

On Vultr and DigitalOcean, destroy the instance and hourly billing stops. Hostinger intro pricing is a prepaid cycle, so you paid for that term up front. Read the refund rules in the cart.
