About this site and how we check prices

We compare VPS, cloud-compute, and managed WordPress products using source-backed product records. The core same-RAM VPS comparison is Hostinger, Vultr, and DigitalOcean; the Finder also tracks managed WordPress products from Hostinger, Kinsta, and WP Engine.

What this site is

An independent buying tool built around a product database. The core same-RAM VPS path covers Hostinger KVM, Vultr Cloud Compute, and DigitalOcean Basic Droplets. The Finder can also compare managed WordPress plans from Hostinger, Kinsta, and WP Engine when the user wants the provider to handle server operations.

Some outbound buttons are affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate participation is stored separately from product facts and does not determine whether a product can exist in the database or rank in the Finder.

How we check prices

The status is date-aware: “verified today” is shown only when the underlying verified product price was actually checked today. Partial data remains partial.

Each product and price observation carries a source, verification status, and date. Our operating target is a daily price pass for tracked commercial products, but the page never advances a date just because a new day started. If a product was not checked today, its actual last-verified date remains visible; if verification is incomplete, it remains marked partial.

  • Verified today: Hostinger VPS, Hostinger managed WordPress, DigitalOcean Basic Droplets, Kinsta managed WordPress, and WP Engine Essential products in the current bootstrap dataset.
  • Partial: Vultr price records remain partial until the live catalog or API is directly verified; billing mechanics are backed by Vultr documentation.
  • Current product database: 21 products across 5 providers, with managed WordPress and game-server intent now modeled separately from raw VPS specs.
  • Price history: dated snapshots are appended instead of overwriting prior observations.

We do not invent missing RAM, vCPU, visit limits, or regions. Managed WordPress plans are compared by the capacity metrics providers actually publish. Game-server RAM sizing is an editorial baseline and is labeled as such, not presented as a benchmark.

What we do not rank for

This site is not an encyclopedia of AWS, Alibaba Cloud, CN2, or mainland-China routing. The product database is expanding by verified buyer intent and product types rather than by publishing every possible cloud page at once.

Start at the VPS Finder, the cheap VPS guide, or the Hostinger, Vultr, and DigitalOcean reviews.
