Hostinger VPS Review 2026: Pricing, Best Uses, and Trade-offs

Hostinger's KVM VPS line is strongest when you want an always-on Linux server at a low prepaid monthly-equivalent price. It is less attractive for hourly, short-lived, or Windows workloads. We track intro and renewal separately so the cheap sticker price is not the whole story.

Verdict

Overall takeStrong value for an always-on VPS
Best forWordPress, self-hosted apps, game servers
Starting price$6.49/mo intro · $11.99 renewal
Weakest fitHourly, ephemeral, or Windows workloads

Hostinger is a value-first VPS choice, not a cloud-compute replacement for every workload. If the server will stay online for months and you are comfortable with a prepaid term, KVM 1 and KVM 2 deliver a lot of RAM and NVMe storage for the advertised monthly-equivalent price. If you create and destroy instances frequently, need true hourly billing, or want a deeper cloud platform around the VM, Vultr or DigitalOcean is usually the cleaner fit.

Bottom line:
Buy Hostinger for an always-on Linux VPS when long-term cost matters more than hourly flexibility. Do not buy it just because the first number is lower: check the prepaid term, renewal, location, and your willingness to manage a server.

Who should buy Hostinger VPS?

Good fit

  • An always-on WordPress, WooCommerce, API, automation, or self-hosted application.
  • You care more about long-term monthly-equivalent cost than hourly flexibility.
  • You want root access, hPanel for account/VPS administration, and Hostinger's AI-assisted VPS tools.
  • You can choose a suitable Hostinger VPS region before setup.

Skip it when...

  • You only need a VM for days or a few weeks and want billing to stop when you destroy it.
  • You routinely create, resize, clone, and delete cloud instances.
  • You need Windows VPS.
  • You want fully managed WordPress and do not want to administer Linux at all.

What Hostinger VPS actually costs

The important distinction is monthly-equivalent price vs billing model. Hostinger's public VPS page shows a low monthly figure, but the plan is prepaid. That is different from Vultr or DigitalOcean, where an instance can be billed by time used and deleted when you no longer need it.

What the sticker price means

  • Intro price: the promotional monthly-equivalent for the initial prepaid term.
  • Renewal: a separately published monthly-equivalent for the next term.
  • Upfront billing: Hostinger says all current VPS plans are paid upfront.
  • Backups: Hostinger's current VPS page lists free weekly backups.

What it does not mean

  • It is not a true $6.49 hourly cloud instance.
  • It does not mean the renewal stays at $6.49.
  • It does not make a 4 GB KVM 1 directly comparable with a 1 GB $6 cloud VM.
  • It does not remove the need to manage your operating system and applications.

Server locations

Hostinger currently lists KVM and Minecraft VPS locations across Europe, Asia, North America, and Brazil. Its latest support documentation lists France, Germany, Lithuania, the United Kingdom and the Netherlands in Europe; India, Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore in Asia; Arizona, Massachusetts and New York in the United States; and Brazil in South America.

Important: choose location deliberately. Hostinger says a VPS location is fixed after setup. Moving later requires reinstalling the VPS in another location, which deletes existing server data, backups and snapshots. Availability can also change, so the final setup screen in hPanel is the source of truth.

Management and control

Hostinger gives you a KVM VPS with root-level control plus hPanel for account and VPS administration. Its current VPS offer also includes an AI web terminal/Hostinger Agent designed to help install, configure and troubleshoot software. That lowers the management burden, but it is still not the same product as fully managed WordPress hosting.

If you want a server where the hosting company owns most of the WordPress maintenance stack, compare the managed WordPress options in the Finder. If you want complete server control and are comfortable managing Linux, Hostinger VPS is the more flexible product.

Performance: what we know and what we do not

Hostinger's current VPS pages advertise AMD EPYC processors, NVMe storage, 1 Gbps networking, KVM virtualization and fixed plan resource limits. Those are useful product facts, but they are not a substitute for an independent benchmark.

VPSComparison benchmark status: we have not yet published our own controlled Hostinger VPS benchmark run. Until we do, this review does not assign a CPU, disk or network performance grade. The recommendation is based on verified product facts, price, billing, region and management fit.

What we do not like

  • Prepaid billing reduces flexibility. Great for a server that stays online; weak for experiments that may disappear next week.
  • Renewal matters. KVM 1 moves from $6.49 to $11.99 monthly-equivalent at renewal, and the larger plans have their own renewal steps.
  • No Windows VPS. Buyers who require Windows should exclude Hostinger before ranking alternatives.
  • Location changes are destructive. A later VPS move requires reinstalling in another location, so backup planning matters.
  • No independent VPSComparison performance benchmark yet. We will not turn vendor specifications into a fake performance score.

FAQs

How much does Hostinger VPS cost?

As verified on 4 Sep 2026, KVM 1 is $6.49/month intro and renews at $11.99; KVM 2 is $8.99 and renews at $14.99; KVM 4 is $12.99 and renews at $28.99; KVM 8 is $25.99 and renews at $49.99. The displayed monthly rate is based on an upfront term, so confirm checkout.

Is Hostinger VPS billed hourly?

No. It is a prepaid VPS product rather than true hourly cloud billing. If you may delete the server quickly, compare Vultr or DigitalOcean.

Is Hostinger VPS good for WordPress?

Yes for an always-on site when you are comfortable administering a VPS. If you do not want to manage Linux, compare fully managed WordPress instead.

Does Hostinger offer Windows VPS?

No. Hostinger currently offers Linux-based VPS templates, not Windows VPS.

Can I change the VPS location later?

Hostinger says the VPS location is fixed after setup. Moving requires reinstalling in another location, which deletes current server data, backups and snapshots, so back up first.

Is Hostinger better than Vultr or DigitalOcean?

It depends on the job. Hostinger is attractive for an always-on prepaid VPS. Vultr and DigitalOcean are usually better fits for hourly, frequently created or deleted instances and cloud-native workflows.
