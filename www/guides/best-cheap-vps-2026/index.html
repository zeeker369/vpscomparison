What not to compare

1 GB badges. Vultr Regular 1 GB is about $5. High Performance 1 GB is $6. DigitalOcean 1 GB is $6. They look cheaper than Hostinger. They are not 4 GB.

Hyperscalers. AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud sell virtual machines inside a cloud account. Bandwidth is often extra. They are not this checkout path.

Annual coupon hosts. Flash deals under $3/month usually mean 1–2 GB, old disks, or a year prepaid. This site does not send signups there.

Hetzner. Useful as a Europe compute filter if your visitors are in the EU. There is no cash affiliate here, so it is not a homepage button. Open Hostinger, Vultr, or DigitalOcean first unless Europe is the actual job.

Common pitfalls

Comparing intro to list. Hostinger’s $5.19 is a first term. Vultr and DigitalOcean $24 is the list price every month.

Ignoring renewal. Hostinger KVM 1 renews at about $11.99/month. Still below 4 GB hourly, but not the badge on the ads.

Buying 1 GB for WordPress. PHP and MySQL will swap. Start at 4 GB.

Backups. Snapshots and backups are often extra. Check the plan page in the cart.
